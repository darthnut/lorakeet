Settings

How Lorakeet itself behaves. Change what you like, then Save: alert settings apply at once; the others are written to lorakeet.toml together (comments and anything else in it kept, the previous copy kept as a backup) and apply after a restart. Your radio's own settings are on the Radio page; other Lorakeets and peers on Stations.
