Over the network you get every decoded packet, but not the per-reception detail (duplicate copies, relays, airtime): radios send their debug log only over USB.

After saving, the Radio page checks your radio's settings (region, per-reception detail and more), sets up channels and confirms logging.

2. This station (optional)

Places this station on the maps and helps estimate where radios without a position are. Best: the antenna's own coordinates (from a map app, or the radio's GPS). A computer's location can be off by a few hundred metres, or by a whole country on a computer with no Wi-Fi. Stays with your own Lorakeets (a peer hub gets it rounded to about 3 km).
