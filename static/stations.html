Stations

Several Lorakeets can log into one database: a hub collects what its stations hear (a Raspberry Pi in the garage, a car, a friend's house). Each station keeps logging on its own when it can't reach the hub, and catches up when it can.

Just one radio on this computer? You don't need anything on this page. Two words used here: a station is one of your Lorakeets sending everything it logs to your hub; a peer is someone else's hub that you trade data with, each of you choosing what the other sees.

Stations

Last contact: when the station last sent anything (red after 10 minutes). Backlog: rows it has logged but not sent yet; it grows while the station is offline and drains when it's back. Connected by: its pairing code, the older shared token, or the peer it arrived from.

Add a station

Install Lorakeet on the station, then give it a pairing code from here. Each code is for one station: the first one to use it claims it, and you can revoke it any time.

Peers: sharing with other hubs

Two people with their own hubs can share what they log. Each direction is separate and set up by the side that sends; the receiving hub has to agree by giving you a peer code. By default a peer gets every reception, position, telemetry and node your stations logged, and text on public channels, not text on private channels or in direct messages (those packets go without their words), and every location rounded to about 3 km. Your stations' own logs (connections, settings, power) stay here.

Hubs this one sends to

They see: what that hub gets (change it any time; it applies to what's sent from then on). Pass on: also forward what other peers shared with you. Waiting: rows not sent to it yet. Pause stops sending for now; nothing is skipped, and on Resume it catches up. Remove stops sending for good; what they already have stays with them.

Send to another hub

The other hub's owner opens their Stations page → Peers → Let another hub send here, and gives you the code.

Hubs that send here

Their stations appear in your station picker and maps like your own. Pause declines what they send for now (it waits on their side and arrives when you resume); Revoke stops them sending more; Delete what it sent also removes everything that arrived from them (it shows exactly what first), leaving your own logs untouched.
